Privacy

Effective October 29, 2026 · This page is versioned in the open-source repo.

The short version: Lararium collects nothing from you. Not because of a setting you have to find — because there is no code that could. No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no phones home.

In Google Play terms: Lararium collects no data and shares no data.

What we don't collect

Lararium's developer never receives your messages, voice recordings, credentials, usage statistics, device identifiers, crash reports, or location. We don't have servers for it, and the software works without our infrastructure entirely. If a Lararium feature ever needed the network, it talks to your machines or to endpoints you configured — never to us.

Where your data actually lives

  • Your conversations, memory, and persona files live on the machine running your hearthd daemon — hardware you own or rent. Backups and deletion are yours to run.
  • Voice is transcribed on your phone by default (an on-device recognizer). Cloud transcription would be an option you enable, clearly labeled, never a silent fallback.
  • Chats go to the model you point Lararium at. Run it all on your own hardware and nothing leaves the house. If you configure a third-party model provider, your prompts go to that provider, under their privacy policy — that is a choice you make in settings, and we call it out there too.
  • Credentials and connector secrets are stored on your hearth, used by name at the wire, and never enter a model's context.

App permissions

The Android app asks for microphone (voice input) and, if you enable them, calendar, contacts, notifications, or call log. Every one of those is read on your device and only crosses the encrypted tunnel to your own daemon if you connect that rail. Permissions you don't grant simply turn features off; the app has no feature it refuses to work without.

This website

lararium.io is a static site with no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no analytics. Like any public website, your browser sends a standard request (IP address, user agent) to the web host, Cloudflare, whose logging is outside our hands and beyond our needs.

Children

Lararium is a self-hosted tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the diff is public in the repo like everything else we do. We don't expect this page to grow — that's rather the point.

Contact

Questions, or proof that something here isn't true: security@lararium.io, or open an issue on GitHub.
